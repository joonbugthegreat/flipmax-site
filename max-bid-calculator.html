How to work out a max bid

Work backward from the sale, not forward from the asking price.

  1. Estimate the sale price. Find several similar cars for sale near you. Take the middle asking price and take a little off, because cars usually sell for less than they are listed for.
  2. Add up every cost. Repairs, title, fees, a detail, tires. Small costs are the ones that get forgotten.
  3. Pick your profit first. Choose the number that makes the car worth your time.
  4. Subtract. Sale price minus costs minus profit is your max bid. Pay that or less, or walk away.

Example: a car that can sell for $8,000, needs $700 in repairs and $300 in fees, with a $1,500 profit target, has a max bid of $5,500.

Three things that make the number wrong

  • Using one listing as the sale price. One high asking price tells you little. Use several.
  • Ignoring the miles. A high mile car does not sell for what a low mile one does. Many listings leave the miles out, so check them yourself.
  • Raising your max bid at the car. Set the number at home. If the seller wants more, negotiate from what you find on the test drive, or leave.

Questions

What is a max bid on a car?

Your max bid is the most you can pay for a car and still reach the profit you want after repairs, fees and other costs. If the seller will not go that low, you walk away.

How do you calculate the max bid for a car you plan to flip?

Start with what the car can sell for. Subtract your repairs, your fees and title costs, any other costs, and the profit you want. What is left is your max bid.

What sell price should I use?

Look at several similar cars listed near you, same model, close in year and miles. Use the middle of those asking prices and take a little off, because asking prices are not selling prices.

How much profit should I aim for on a flip?

That is your call. Pick a number that is worth your time and the risk on that car, and put it in before you look at the asking price. No calculator can promise a profit.

Is the calculator free?

Yes. It runs in your browser, needs no account, and the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.

Do I need a dealer license to flip cars?

That depends on your state. Most states limit how many cars a person can sell in a year without one. Check your state's rule.
