Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

The short version

FlipMax is operated by Stacy Maxxing LLC, a Colorado company. The app has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.

  • Stays on your phone: your cars, deal checks, garage, profit history, notes, settings and any car photos you take.
  • Leaves your phone: the car details and ZIP code needed to look up prices, a random ID used to count your lookups and searches, a creator code if you enter one, the chat text if you use Lightning Help, and purchase information handled by Apple.
  • We do not sell your data and the app has no ads.

Website waitlist

If you enter your email on flipmax.app to get early access, we store that email on our server host and use it only to tell you when the app is available. It is not linked to anything you do in the app, and we do not sell it. Email us to be removed.

Optional questions

FlipMax may ask where you heard about the app, and, after your first Deal Check, whether the max bid was useful. Both can be skipped. If you answer, we store your answer, anything you type in the optional creator name or code box, the dollar amount you type if you tell us what max bid you expected, and the year, make and model of the car you had just checked, together with the date and time and a random install ID that the app creates on your phone. The install ID is not your name, email address or account, and we do not link these answers to who you are. We use them only to learn which channels bring people to FlipMax and to make the max bid better. We do not sell them or use them for advertising.

Screenshot check (optional)

If you choose Check a screenshot, you pick one image from your photos with your phone's photo picker. FlipMax only receives the image you pick, never your other photos. The image is sent over a secure connection to a service provider that reads images for us. It reads the car details shown, such as the year, make, model, price and mileage, and sends them back to the app, where you can correct them before your Deal Check. We do not save the image. It is used only to read the listing and is not kept by FlipMax after the request ends. We keep a daily count of screenshot checks for each random install ID to prevent misuse, but not the image or what it showed. Please do not pick screenshots that show other people's personal information.

Morning alerts (optional)

If you turn on the morning alert in Profile, FlipMax asks your phone for permission to send notifications. If you allow it, we store a push token (an identifier for your phone that Apple's push service and our push provider, Expo, use to deliver notifications), the ZIP code and search distance you use in the app, your phone's time zone, and a random install ID that FlipMax creates on your phone, together with the time this was last updated. We use this only to send you alerts about new cars near your ZIP. The token and install ID are not your name, email address or account. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising. Turning the switch off stops the alerts, and you can also turn notifications off for FlipMax in your phone's Settings at any time. We share the push token with Expo and Apple only to deliver the alert.

Saved cars and price drops

When you save a car, FlipMax keeps it on your phone: the listing's id and web link, the car's name, and the last price we saw. You can save up to 20 cars. So that we can tell you when a saved car's price drops, we also store the listing id and last price seen on our server, together with a random install ID that FlipMax creates on your phone. The install ID is not your name, email address or account. We delete the server copy when you remove the car from your saved cars, and we use it only to check for price drops. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Reminders

If you turn on the evening reminder, our server also keeps the time you last opened the Deals screen, so we do not remind you after you have already looked. Garage reminders are set on your phone only: FlipMax does not send your Garage cars to us for them.

Sharing to FlipMax

If you share a link or text to FlipMax from another app, FlipMax uses the text on your phone to fill in a car's year, make and model, and keeps the link with that car on your phone so you can open the listing yourself later. FlipMax does not send the link or the text to us. If the optional list of cars to check is turned on, your phone asks the linked page itself, once, for its public preview (title, price and picture), the same way a browser or a chat app shows a link preview. That request goes from your phone straight to that website, which sees it like any visitor, and it does not go through us. If you share an image, it is handled like a screenshot check: it is read to find the car's details and is not stored.

Links to other websites

FlipMax shows links that open listings, searches and seller pages on other websites and apps, including eBay and Facebook Marketplace. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we do not control what they collect. FlipMax is not affiliated with eBay, Facebook or Meta.

Lightning Help (AI chat)

Lightning Help is an in-app chat helper that uses AI from Anthropic. The first time you open it, the app tells you this before you can send a message. What you type is sent through our server (hosted by Supabase) to Anthropic only to write an answer. We do not save your messages or the answers on our server, and the app does not send your location, ZIP code or VIN unless you type one into the chat. Please do not share passwords, card numbers or your exact address. Our server keeps only a count of how many messages a random ID sent that day, to enforce a daily limit. Your chat is kept only on your phone, and Clear chat deletes it. Answers are tips and estimates, not advice.

Keeping and deleting your information

On your phone. Everything stored on your phone stays until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes it.

On our server. We keep only lookup, search and Lightning message counts tied to a random ID, and a creator code if you entered one. We do not keep the vehicles you looked up or the listings returned. Counts are deleted on a regular schedule.

Asking us to delete. Because the app has no accounts, we usually cannot tell which random ID is yours. If you want your information removed, email us and we will tell you how to find the ID in the app.

Children

The app is for adults who buy and sell vehicles. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to not be treated differently for asking. Email us to make a request.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant we will say so in the app.
