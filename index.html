Why good looking deals turn into losses

You are not bad at flipping. The numbers you were given are wrong for flipping.

  • Asking prices are not selling prices. The listings you compare against are what sellers hope for, not what a car sells for this week.
  • Book values run high on high mile cars. A 250,000 mile sedan does not sell for what a price guide says.
  • The small costs get forgotten. Title, fees, a set of tires and a detail can eat the whole profit.

How FlipMax gets to one number

It works backward. FlipMax starts with the price the car should sell at fast in your area, then takes off your fees, your repairs and the profit you want. What is left is the most you should pay. If the seller wants more, you negotiate or walk.
