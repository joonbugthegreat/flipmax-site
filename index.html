Why good looking deals turn into losses

You are not bad at flipping. It is easy to start from the wrong number.

01

Asking prices are not selling prices

One high listing tells you little. FlipMax takes the middle of several similar asking prices and takes a little off, and the real sale can still differ.

02

Miles change everything

A 250,000 mile car does not sell for what a low mile one does. Many listings leave the miles out, so an estimate is often not adjusted for them. Check the miles yourself.

03

The small costs get forgotten

Title, fees, a set of tires and a detail can eat the whole profit.

How FlipMax gets to one number

It works backward. FlipMax estimates what the car can sell for from similar cars listed near you, then takes off your fees, your repairs and the profit you want. What is left is the most you should pay. If the seller wants more, you negotiate or walk. You can try the math yourself with the free max bid calculator or the profit calculator. FlipMax aims for 5 similar cars. It looks at cars within 2 model years first, then widens to 4 model years and then to a wider area. With only 3 or 4 similar cars it can still show an estimate, marked low confidence, and it says how many cars it used. With fewer than 3 it says there are not enough local sales to price it, and you can type your own price.

You put in your own costs: repairs, fees, title, detailing and the profit you want. FlipMax shows the math in one line. It also warns you when a price is far below market, so you check the car, the title and the seller before sending money.

Questions

Is it really free to try?

Yes. 5 searches and 1 full Deal Check with no card and no account.

Where do the cars come from?

Public Facebook Marketplace listings near you from the last 30 days, written by their sellers. Price estimates come from the asking prices of similar listings. FlipMax is not affiliated with Facebook or Meta, and we do not own, sell or inspect the cars.

Is the profit guaranteed?

No. Every price, profit number and rating is an estimate. Always inspect the car and check the title yourself before you buy.

What phones does it work on?

iPhone at launch, in the United States.

Do I need a dealer license?

That depends on your state. Most states limit how many cars a person can sell in a year without one. Check your state's rule.

How do I cancel?

Any time, in your Apple Account settings under Subscriptions.
