How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, tap FlipMax, then tap Cancel Subscription. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the FlipMax purchase.

I paid but my plan is not showing

Open the plans screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with. If it still does not show, email us.

Where do the car values come from?

From public Facebook Marketplace listings near you from the last 30 days. FlipMax looks at the asking prices of similar cars and aims for 5 of them. It first looks at cars within 2 model years of yours nearby, then within 4 model years, then within 4 model years over a wider area, and stops at the first step that finds 5. With only 3 or 4 similar cars it still shows an estimate, marked low confidence, and says how many cars it used. With fewer than 3 it gives no estimate. Listings often leave out mileage, so a value may not be adjusted for it. FlipMax is not affiliated with Facebook or Meta. Values and the max bid are estimates to help you decide, not a guarantee of what a car will sell for. Always check the car, the title and the seller yourself.

Why is the value different from another site?

Different tools use different listings and methods. FlipMax looks at similar cars listed for sale near you and lets you add your own costs and target profit, so the max bid fits your deal.

How do I delete my information?
