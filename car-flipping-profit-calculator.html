How to read the result

  • Check the low case. The calculator also shows your profit if the car sells for 10 percent less. If that turns into a loss, the deal is thin.
  • Count your time. A small profit on a car that takes weeks of work and selling may not be worth it.
  • Work backward next time. Decide the profit you want first, then find the most you can pay with the max bid calculator.

Questions

How do you calculate profit on a car flip?

Take what the car sells for. Subtract the price you paid, your repairs, and your fees like title, registration and listing costs. What is left is your profit before taxes.

Why check a lower sale price?

Cars often sell for less than you hope, or take longer to sell. Checking a lower price shows whether the flip still works if that happens.

Does this include taxes?

No. The result is before income taxes. Ask a tax professional how flipping income is taxed where you live.

Is the calculator free?

Yes. It runs in your browser, needs no account, and the numbers you type are not sent anywhere.
